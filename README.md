## Data
HR employee data containing more than 22,000 records collected between 2000 and 2020.

## Technologies Used
- Data Cleaning and Analysis: MySQL
- Data Visualization: Power BI

## Summary of Findings

- Male employees slightly outnumber female employees.
- White employees form the largest racial group, while Native Hawaiian and American Indian employees form the smallest groups.
- The youngest employee is 23 years old, while the oldest employee is 60 years old.
- Employees were divided into five age groups (18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, and 55–64). Most employees belong to the 25–54 age range, while relatively few employees fall into the 18–24 age group.
- Most employees work at headquarters rather than remotely.
- The average employment duration for terminated employees is approximately 8 years.
- The gender distribution across departments is fairly balanced, although male employees slightly outnumber female employees.
- The Auditing department has the highest turnover rate, followed by the Legal department, while the Marketing department has the lowest turnover rate.
- A large proportion of employees are located in the state of Ohio.
- The net change in the number of employees has increased over the years.
- The average tenure across departments is approximately 9 years.
